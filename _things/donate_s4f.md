Support independent science and help fight climate change! Scientists4Future is a German initiative that empowers scientists to engage with the public and policymakers. Your donation can make a big difference.  

By contributing, you help fund research, public education campaigns, and advocacy that drives real change in climate policy. Every donation, no matter the size, strengthens the voice of science in society and supports evidence-based solutions for a sustainable future.

You can donate directly here: [Donate to Scientists4Future](https://de.scientists4future.org/spenden/)

Together, we can ensure that science remains a guiding force in shaping a healthier planet for generations to come.
